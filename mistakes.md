# Mistakes

Recurring mistakes, grouped by type. Each example shows the original, the correction, and the session it came from.

## Articles (a / the / none)
- "the Earth" → **on Earth** (no "the" in "on Earth") — [2026-11-04 bepicolombo-moon-chat](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- "the people" for people in general → **people** — 2026-11-04
- "this Spacecraft tour" → **a spacecraft tour** (use "a" for a general thing, not "this") — 2026-11-04
- "the moon" for Earth's moon → **the Moon** (with "the", capital M) — 2026-11-04

## Verb forms and subject-verb agreement
- "should solved" → **should solve** (base verb after a modal) — 2026-11-04
- "Elon Musk have" → **Elon Musk has** (he/she/it takes "has") — 2026-11-04
- "would be covince" → **would be convinced** (past participle after "be") — 2026-11-04
- "Not really know" → **I don't really know** (missing subject and "do") — 2026-11-04
- "explore on space is..." → **exploring space is...** (a verb as a subject becomes -ing) — 2026-11-04
- "there is a lot of ppl" → **there are a lot of people** — 2026-11-04

## Word choice and collocations
- "have this purchase" → **make this purchase** (or just "buy") — 2026-11-04
- "bad things always made from people" → **are caused by people** — 2026-11-04
- "evidence points it was fake" → **evidence suggests / some people say...** ("points" needs "to"; "evidence" is uncountable) — 2026-11-04

## Prepositions
- "idea on it" → **ideas about it** — 2026-11-04
- "explore on space" → **explore space** (no preposition) — 2026-11-04

## Spelling and capitalization
- Amstrong → **Armstrong**, Goverments → **Governments**, thins → **things**, covince → **convinced**, ppl → **people** — 2026-11-04
- "Spacecraft" in the middle of a sentence → **spacecraft** — 2026-11-04

## Sentence structure
- Answering with a fragment ("moon first.") → use a full sentence with a subject and a verb — 2026-11-04
- Using Chinese when stuck (證據, 逃避, 我能力內可以購買的) → ask for the English word; good new words: **evidence**, **escape / escapism**, **what I can afford** — 2026-11-04
