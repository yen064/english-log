# Phrases

The phrases of the day (1 to 5 per session), alphabetical. Each entry links to Google Translate and back to the session file where it was taught.

- **hitch a ride** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=hitch%20a%20ride&op=translate)): to get a free trip in someone's car or on something that moves (搭便車) — casual — [2026-11-04 Chihuahua Riding a Tortoise](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **out of this world** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=out%20of%20this%20world&op=translate)): extremely good or amazing (棒極了、非比尋常) — casual — [2026-11-04 BepiColombo and the Moon](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **put up with** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=put%20up%20with&op=translate)): to accept a bad or annoying situation without complaining (忍受) — neutral — [2026-11-04 Chihuahua Riding a Tortoise](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **Seeing is believing** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Seeing%20is%20believing&op=translate)): you only believe something after you see it yourself (眼見為憑) — neutral, an idiom — [2026-11-04 BepiColombo and the Moon](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **the sky is the limit** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=the%20sky%20is%20the%20limit&op=translate)): there is no limit to what you can do or get (沒有上限、一切皆有可能) — casual — [2026-11-04 BepiColombo and the Moon](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **There's nothing I can do about it.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=There%27s%20nothing%20I%20can%20do%20about%20it&op=translate)): I can't change the situation (我對此無能為力) — neutral — [2026-11-04 Chihuahua Riding a Tortoise](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
