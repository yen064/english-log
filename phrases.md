# Phrases

The phrases of the day (1 to 5 per session), alphabetical. Each entry links to Google Translate, and ends with links to the session files where it was taught: [1] is the first session, [2] the second, and so on.

- **First things first.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=First%20things%20first&op=translate)): do the most important thing before anything else (先做最重要的事) — casual — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **hitch a ride** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=hitch%20a%20ride&op=translate)): to get a free trip in someone's car or on something that moves (搭便車) — casual — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **I don't get it.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=I%20don%27t%20get%20it&op=translate)): I don't understand (我不懂) — casual — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **I wouldn't tell you if I did know.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=I%20wouldn%27t%20tell%20you%20if%20I%20did%20know&op=translate)): I know, or maybe I don't, but I would not say it either way (就算我知道，我也不會告訴你) — casual, a little rude — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **out of this world** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=out%20of%20this%20world&op=translate)): extremely good or amazing (棒極了、非比尋常) — casual — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **put up with** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=put%20up%20with&op=translate)): to accept a bad or annoying situation without complaining (忍受) — neutral — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **Seeing is believing** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Seeing%20is%20believing&op=translate)): you only believe something after you see it yourself (眼見為憑) — neutral, an idiom — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **the sky is the limit** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=the%20sky%20is%20the%20limit&op=translate)): there is no limit to what you can do or get (沒有上限、一切皆有可能) — casual — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
