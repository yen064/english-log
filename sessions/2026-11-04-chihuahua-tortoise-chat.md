## New vocabulary
- **unbothered** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=unbothered&op=translate)): calm and not affected by something annoying (不受影響、毫不在意的) — The cat looked unbothered by the loud noise.
- **go viral** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=go%20viral&op=translate)): to spread very fast online (爆紅、瘋傳) — The video of the tortoise went viral overnight.
- **hitch a ride** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=hitch%20a%20ride&op=translate)): to get a free trip in someone's car or on something that moves (搭便車) — My car broke down, so I hitched a ride with a stranger.
- **hitchhike** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=hitchhike&op=translate)): to travel by asking drivers for free rides on the road (在路邊攔便車旅行) — He hitchhiked across the country.
- **give someone a ride** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=give%20someone%20a%20ride&op=translate)): to take someone in your car (載某人一程) — Can you give me a ride to the station?
- **tortoise** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=tortoise&op=translate)): a land turtle with a high, round shell (陸龜) — The tortoise walked slowly across the kitchen.
- **Chihuahua** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Chihuahua&op=translate)): a very small dog breed from Mexico, pronounced chi-WAH-wah (吉娃娃) — My neighbor has a brave little Chihuahua.
- **There's nothing I can do about it.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=There%27s%20nothing%20I%20can%20do%20about%20it&op=translate)): I can't change the situation (我對此無能為力) — The flight is late, and there's nothing I can do about it.
- **I have no choice.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=I%20have%20no%20choice&op=translate)): I must accept it (我別無選擇) — I have no choice, so I'll work this weekend.
- **put up with** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=put%20up%20with&op=translate)): to accept a bad situation without complaining (忍受) — I can't put up with this noise anymore.
- **swear word** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=swear%20word&op=translate)): a rude or offensive word; also called a curse word (髒話) — He said a swear word when he dropped his phone.
- **profanity** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=profanity&op=translate)): very rude or offensive language (粗話、褻瀆的言語) — The movie has a lot of profanity.
- **Get off me!** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Get%20off%20me&op=translate)): move away from my body (從我身上下去！) — Get off me, you're too heavy!
- **Are you kidding me?** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Are%20you%20kidding%20me&op=translate)): said when you are surprised or annoyed (你開玩笑嗎？) — Are you kidding me? The bus left without us?
- **What the heck?** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=What%20the%20heck&op=translate)): a mild way to say "What the hell?" when you are surprised or annoyed (搞什麼鬼？) — What the heck? Who ate my lunch?
