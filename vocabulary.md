# Vocabulary

Alphabetical. Each entry links to Google Translate. Details and context are in the session files.

- **afford** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=afford&op=translate)): to have enough money (or time) to buy or do something (負擔得起) — I can't afford a one-million-dollar ticket.
- **be caused by** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=be%20caused%20by&op=translate)): to happen because of something (由…造成) — The accident was caused by bad weather.
- **be scheduled to** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=be%20scheduled%20to&op=translate)): planned to happen at a certain time (預定要…) — The spacecraft is scheduled to enter orbit on 21 November.
- **conspiracy theory** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=conspiracy%20theory&op=translate)): a belief that a secret group is hiding the truth (陰謀論) — The moon landing conspiracy theory is very popular online.
- **convinced** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=convinced&op=translate)): sure that something is true because of good reasons or evidence (確信、被說服) — I'm convinced that the landing was real.
- **delay** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=delay&op=translate)): to make something happen later than planned (延誤、延後) — Thruster problems delayed the mission by almost a year.
- **enter orbit** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=enter%20orbit&op=translate)): to start moving around a planet on a fixed path (進入軌道) — The spacecraft will enter orbit around Mercury.
- **escapism** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=escapism&op=translate)): avoiding real problems by thinking about or doing something else (逃避現實) — Playing games all night is a kind of escapism.
- **evidence** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=evidence&op=translate)): facts that show something is true; uncountable (證據) — There is a lot of evidence for climate change.
- **fake / faked** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=fake%20%2F%20faked&op=translate)): not real; made to look real (假的、偽造的) — The photo was faked.
- **hoax** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=hoax&op=translate)): a trick that makes people believe something false (騙局) — Some people call the moon landing a hoax.
- **make a purchase** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=make%20a%20purchase&op=translate)): to buy something (購買) — I made a purchase online yesterday.
- **overpopulation** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=overpopulation&op=translate)): too many people living in one place (人口過剩) — Overpopulation can cause food shortages.
- **probe** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=probe&op=translate)): an uncrewed spacecraft sent to study a place (探測器) — BepiColombo is a probe.
- **rocket** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=rocket&op=translate)): the vehicle that launches a spacecraft into space (火箭) — The rocket launched the satellite.
- **rover** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=rover&op=translate)): a vehicle that drives on a planet's surface (探測車) — The rover is exploring Mars.
- **satellite** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=satellite&op=translate)): an object that stays in orbit around a planet (衛星) — Weather satellites help us predict storms.
- **Seeing is believing** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Seeing%20is%20believing&op=translate)): an idiom meaning you only believe something after you see it (眼見為憑) — I didn't trust the photos, but seeing is believing.
- **spacecraft** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=spacecraft&op=translate)): a vehicle built to travel in space; the plural is also "spacecraft" (太空船) — The spacecraft will enter orbit around Mercury next month.
- **spaceship** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=spaceship&op=translate)): a spacecraft, more common in movies and science fiction (太空船) — The spaceship landed on a strange planet.
- **thruster** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=thruster&op=translate)): a small engine that moves or turns a spacecraft (推進器) — Thruster problems delayed the mission.
- **up to** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=up%20to&op=translate)): as much as, but not more than (最多到) — I can pay up to 100 dollars.
- **with my own eyes** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=with%20my%20own%20eyes&op=translate)): personally seeing something yourself (親眼) — I saw the accident with my own eyes.
- **within my budget** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=within%20my%20budget&op=translate)): not more than the money you planned to spend (在預算內) — I'm looking for a laptop within my budget.
