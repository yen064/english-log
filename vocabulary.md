# Vocabulary

Alphabetical. Each entry links to Google Translate. Each entry ends with links to the session files where it appeared: [1] is the first session, [2] the second, and so on.

- **afford** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=afford&op=translate)): to have enough money (or time) to buy or do something (負擔得起) — I can't afford a one-million-dollar ticket. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **Are you kidding me?** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Are%20you%20kidding%20me&op=translate)): said when you are surprised or annoyed (你開玩笑嗎？) — Are you kidding me? The bus left without us? — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **backlash** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=backlash&op=translate)): a strong negative reaction from many people (反彈) — The new rule caused a public backlash. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **be caused by** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=be%20caused%20by&op=translate)): to happen because of something (由…造成) — The accident was caused by bad weather. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **be scheduled to** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=be%20scheduled%20to&op=translate)): planned to happen at a certain time (預定要…) — The spacecraft is scheduled to enter orbit on 21 November. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **brilliant** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=brilliant&op=translate)): very clever or talented (非常聰明的、傑出的) — She is a brilliant scientist. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **Chihuahua** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Chihuahua&op=translate)): a very small dog breed from Mexico, pronounced chi-WAH-wah (吉娃娃) — My neighbor has a brave little Chihuahua. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **conspiracy theory** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=conspiracy%20theory&op=translate)): a belief that a secret group is hiding the truth (陰謀論) — The moon landing conspiracy theory is very popular online. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **convinced** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=convinced&op=translate)): sure that something is true because of good reasons or evidence (確信、被說服) — I'm convinced that the landing was real. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **delay** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=delay&op=translate)): to make something happen later than planned (延誤、延後) — Thruster problems delayed the mission by almost a year. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **enter orbit** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=enter%20orbit&op=translate)): to start moving around a planet on a fixed path (進入軌道) — The spacecraft will enter orbit around Mercury. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **escapism** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=escapism&op=translate)): avoiding real problems by thinking about or doing something else (逃避現實) — Playing games all night is a kind of escapism. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **evidence** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=evidence&op=translate)): facts that show something is true; uncountable (證據) — There is a lot of evidence for climate change. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **fake / faked** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=fake%20%2F%20faked&op=translate)): not real; made to look real (假的、偽造的) — The photo was faked. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **First things first.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=First%20things%20first&op=translate)): do the most important thing before anything else (先做最重要的事) — First things first, let's eat, then we'll talk. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **genius** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=genius&op=translate)): a person with extraordinary intelligence or talent (天才) — Many people called him a genius. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **Get off me!** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Get%20off%20me&op=translate)): move away from my body (從我身上下去！) — Get off me, you're too heavy! — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **give someone a ride** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=give%20someone%20a%20ride&op=translate)): to take someone in your car (載某人一程) — Can you give me a ride to the station? — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **go viral** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=go%20viral&op=translate)): to spread very fast online (爆紅、瘋傳) — The video of the tortoise went viral overnight. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **gridlock** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=gridlock&op=translate)): a situation where nothing can move or be decided, such as in politics (僵局) — Political gridlock stopped the new law. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **hitch a ride** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=hitch%20a%20ride&op=translate)): to get a free trip in someone's car or on something that moves (搭便車) — My car broke down, so I hitched a ride with a stranger. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **hitchhike** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=hitchhike&op=translate)): to travel by asking drivers for free rides on the road (在路邊攔便車旅行) — He hitchhiked across the country. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **hoax** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=hoax&op=translate)): a trick that makes people believe something false (騙局) — Some people call the moon landing a hoax. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **hungry** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=hungry&op=translate)): needing food (餓的) — Many children in the world are still hungry. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **I don't get it.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=I%20don%27t%20get%20it&op=translate)): I don't understand (我不懂) — I don't get it. Can you explain again? — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **I have no choice.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=I%20have%20no%20choice&op=translate)): I must accept it (我別無選擇) — I have no choice, so I'll work this weekend. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **I wouldn't tell you if I did know.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=I%20wouldn%27t%20tell%20you%20if%20I%20did%20know&op=translate)): I know, or maybe I don't, but I would not say it either way (就算我知道，我也不會告訴你) — I wouldn't tell you if I did know where the party is. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **in need** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=in%20need&op=translate)): without enough food, money or help (需要幫助的) — We donate clothes to children in need. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **make a purchase** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=make%20a%20purchase&op=translate)): to buy something (購買) — I made a purchase online yesterday. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **midterm** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=midterm&op=translate)): an election held in the middle of a president's term; also an exam in the middle of a course (期中選舉；期中考) — The midterm election is in November. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **obligation** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=obligation&op=translate)): something you must do because it is right or required (義務、責任) — We have an obligation to help children in need. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **overpopulation** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=overpopulation&op=translate)): too many people living in one place (人口過剩) — Overpopulation can cause food shortages. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **priority** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=priority&op=translate)): something that is more important than other things (優先事項) — Safety is our top priority. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **probe** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=probe&op=translate)): an uncrewed spacecraft sent to study a place (探測器) — BepiColombo is a probe. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **profanity** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=profanity&op=translate)): very rude or offensive language (粗話、褻瀆的言語) — The movie has a lot of profanity. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **put up with** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=put%20up%20with&op=translate)): to accept a bad situation without complaining (忍受) — I can't put up with this noise anymore. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **reporter** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=reporter&op=translate)): a person who asks questions and writes or speaks news for a news organization (記者) — The reporter asked the president a difficult question. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **rocket** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=rocket&op=translate)): the vehicle that launches a spacecraft into space (火箭) — The rocket launched the satellite. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **rover** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=rover&op=translate)): a vehicle that drives on a planet's surface (探測車) — The rover is exploring Mars. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **satellite** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=satellite&op=translate)): an object that stays in orbit around a planet (衛星) — Weather satellites help us predict storms. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **Seeing is believing** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Seeing%20is%20believing&op=translate)): an idiom meaning you only believe something after you see it (眼見為憑) — I didn't trust the photos, but seeing is believing. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **spacecraft** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=spacecraft&op=translate)): a vehicle built to travel in space; the plural is also "spacecraft" (太空船) — The spacecraft will enter orbit around Mercury next month. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **spaceship** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=spaceship&op=translate)): a spacecraft, more common in movies and science fiction (太空船) — The spaceship landed on a strange planet. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **stalemate** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=stalemate&op=translate)): a situation where neither side can win or make progress (僵持) — The talks ended in a stalemate. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **starving** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=starving&op=translate)): extremely hungry, or dying from lack of food (快餓死的) — I'm starving, let's order pizza. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **sticking point** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=sticking%20point&op=translate)): a part of a discussion that is hard to agree on (卡關的爭議點) — Price was the main sticking point in the deal. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **swear word** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=swear%20word&op=translate)): a rude or offensive word; also called a curse word (髒話) — He said a swear word when he dropped his phone. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **There's nothing I can do about it.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=There%27s%20nothing%20I%20can%20do%20about%20it&op=translate)): I can't change the situation (我對此無能為力) — The flight is late, and there's nothing I can do about it. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **thruster** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=thruster&op=translate)): a small engine that moves or turns a spacecraft (推進器) — Thruster problems delayed the mission. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **tortoise** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=tortoise&op=translate)): a land turtle with a high, round shell (陸龜) — The tortoise walked slowly across the kitchen. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **unbothered** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=unbothered&op=translate)): calm and not affected by something annoying (不受影響、毫不在意的) — The cat looked unbothered by the loud noise. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **up to** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=up%20to&op=translate)): as much as, but not more than (最多到) — I can pay up to 100 dollars. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **veto** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=veto&op=translate)): to officially refuse to approve a law or decision (否決) — The president threatened to veto the bill. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **We have all the cards.** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=We%20have%20all%20the%20cards&op=translate)): we have the stronger position in a negotiation (我們握有所有的牌，占上風) — In this deal, we have all the cards. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **What the heck?** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=What%20the%20heck&op=translate)): a mild way to say "What the hell?" when you are surprised or annoyed (搞什麼鬼？) — What the heck? Who ate my lunch? — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-chihuahua-tortoise-chat.md)
- **Who are you with?** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=Who%20are%20you%20with%3F&op=translate)): which news organization or company do you work for? (你是哪一家媒體／公司的？) — "Who are you with?" "I'm with a local newspaper." — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **with my own eyes** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=with%20my%20own%20eyes&op=translate)): personally seeing something yourself (親眼) — I saw the accident with my own eyes. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
- **wither** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=wither&op=translate)): to become weak and slowly die or fade (枯萎、慢慢衰弱) — Without water, the plant started to wither. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-white-house-press-qa-video.md)
- **within my budget** ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=en&tl=zh-TW&text=within%20my%20budget&op=translate)): not more than the money you planned to spend (在預算內) — I'm looking for a laptop within my budget. — [\[1\]](sessions/2026-11-04-bepicolombo-moon-chat.md)
